A complete, original open-world crime game built for
[make.fun's gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) ($1,666).
Dario Reyes, a driver for hire in the sun-soaked coastal city of Solmara,
takes jobs from fixer Mama Rosa — steal cars, run packages, tail couriers,
lose the heat, and pull the big score. All original: our own city,
characters, vehicles, story, art, sound and music. Nothing from Rockstar.

## How to play

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `index.html` in a desktop
browser — no build step, no network requests, all sounds synthesized with
WebAudio.

### On foot
- **WASD / Arrow keys** — move
- **Mouse** — aim · **Click / Space** — shoot pistol · **F** — punch
- **E** — steal / enter the nearest car

### Driving
- **W / Up** — accelerate · **S / Down** — brake / reverse
- **A / D** — steer · **Space** — handbrake
- **E** — get out · **F** — (on foot) punch

### Always
- **P / Esc** — pause · **M** — mute · **R** — restart after busted/wasted
- **Enter / Click** — start, advance mission briefings

## The city

Solmara is a 3.6 km open world: a street grid with traffic and pedestrians,
a beach strip, docks, a park, and Rosa's Garage as your hideout. Steal cars,
earn cash from 5 story missions, and watch the wanted stars — the Solmara PD
sends cruisers after you, and they do not give up easily. Lay low or hide at
the garage to cool the heat.

## Missions

1. **First Wheels** — steal a car and bring it to Rosa's Garage.
2. **Package Run** — collect the package at the docks, deliver before time runs out.
3. **Tail Job** — follow the courier without being spotted.
4. **Heat Wave** — lose a 3-star wanted level.
5. **The Big Score** — hit Solmara Bank and escape to the hideout with the cops on you.

Progress, cash and completed missions save to localStorage — pick up where
you left off.
